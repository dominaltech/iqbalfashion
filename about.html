About Iqbal Fashion

Excellence in Bespoke Men's Fashion, Fine Fabrics & Readymade Apparel

Our Story & Vision

Founded in Solapur, Iqbal Fashion was established with a singular devotion to artisanal men's tailoring and fine cloth materials. Specializing in bespoke ethnic wear, modern casual wear, premium unstitched fabrics (Linen, Cotton, Silk, Khadi, Suiting, Shirting), and boys & girls apparel, we believe that true elegance begins with world-class textiles and master cut.

Premium Cloth Materials

We curate purest linen yarns, rich Egyptian & Giza cottons, pure handloom khadi, and luxurious Italian-blend suiting materials chosen for breathability, drape, and enduring elegance.

Bespoke & Readymade

From handcrafted royal sherwanis and festive kurtas to modern smart-casual trousers and blazers, every garment exemplifies meticulous tailoring and timeless silhouette.

Why Choose Iqbal Fashion?

Customers across India trust Iqbal Fashion for our verified fabric authenticity, bespoke tailoring standards, express doorstep delivery, and dedicated customer assistance.
